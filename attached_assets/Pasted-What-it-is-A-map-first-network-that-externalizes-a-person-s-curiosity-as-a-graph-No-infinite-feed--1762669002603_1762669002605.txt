What it is

A map-first network that externalizes a person’s curiosity as a graph. No infinite feed. Core loop = see yourself → adjust → discover resonance → opt-in connect.

Core Objects

Node: an interest (topic, creator, work, tag, behavior-derived theme).

Edge: strength of relationship (co-occur, sequential consumption, shared vocab, temporal coupling).

Cluster: community of nodes; becomes a “domain” (e.g., “HAI & interpretability”).

Signature: the shape of your graph (embedding of the full topology). Used for matching.

Home (Self Map)

Default screen is your graph. It breathes and updates in real time.

Layout: force-directed, gravity = personal salience. Larger nodes = time-weighted attention.

Color: start monochrome for calm. Add color only when you turn on a lens (e.g., recency, mood, location, media type).

Interaction:

Tap a node → side panel: why it’s here (evidence: likes, saves, watch time, highlights), neighbors, drift over time.

Lasso multiple nodes → create a Circuit (your custom theme). Circuits become saved filters and matching units.

Heat toggle → shows where attention is spiking or decaying this week.

Governance UI: every node has a “because” line and a Kill/Keep control. Kill removes/weights down a theme; Keep “pins” it.

Discovery (Resonance, not Virality)

Core verb: “Find resonance.” No one’s identity until both sides consent.

Near Resonance: people within X km whose graph signature is close to yours overall.

Circuit Resonance: people whose graphs are dissimilar overall but highly aligned on one Circuit (useful for cross-disciplinary serendipity).

Bridge Seek: find users who bridge two of your clusters you’ve never bridged. This grows you.

Reveal mechanic (double-consent):

You see a Resonance Card: “Strong match on: Narrative cognition ↔ AI safety (3 circuits).”

If you tap Invite Reveal, the other side gets a symmetric card. Identity only unlocks on mutual reveal.

Real-Time Without Doomscroll

Ticker, not feed. A slim, dismissible strip shows state changes: “Your ‘Systems + Story’ circuit gained 2 new bridges,” “Local Pulse: ‘Compute efficiency’ trending.”

Session tasks: Each session offers 3 curiosity actions (e.g., “Explore one new node from a quiet corner,” “Bridge two clusters”).

Hard stop: App nudges you out after ~5–10 actions. No endless scroll by design.

Matching Logic (High Level, implementable)

Build node embeddings: from text + behavior (likes, dwell time, saves). Use sentence-level transformer + time decay.

Graph embedding: graph2vec/GraphSAGE on your personal graph → signature vector.

Resonance score:

Global: cosine(signature_A, signature_B).

Circuit-specific: Jaccard/overlap on node sets + mean edge similarity.

Bridge score: count of high-betweenness candidates that connect two user clusters.

Diversity guardrails: penalize matches that are too similar across all circuits; boost cross-cluster complementarity with at least one high-overlap circuit.

Privacy & Agency (Non-negotiable)

Local Pulse is k-anonymous + DP noise. No single user reconstructable.

Selective visibility: users choose which Circuits are discoverable. Private by default.

Ephemeral matching windows: reveal invites expire; no inbox hoarding.

Growth & Empathy Features

Arc View (Timeline): scrub a slider and watch your graph morph month by month. You see identity as a trajectory, not a label.

Mirror Mode: compare “today vs. 90 days ago” (which clusters expanded/atrophied, which edges appeared).

Perspective Swap (opt-in): temporarily view a synthetic approximation of someone else’s graph (structure only). You learn how they think without personal content.

Incentive Design (Make curiosity the currency)

No public like counts. Replace with Resonance gained and Bridges created.

Creator surfaces: if your post creates new edges between nodes in many users’ graphs, it’s highlighted. Reward = connectivity impact, not raw views.

Anti-Abuse & Health

Adaptive friction: cold outreach locked; everything routes through double-consent reveal.

Toxicity/autocult detectors: clusters with extreme insularity and high emotional arousal + low bridge rate trigger gentle curiosity prompts.

Rate limits: the app ends a session by design. You come to tend your graph, not live inside it.

Onboarding & Data Ingest (MVP)

Start lightweight: manual seeds (5 interests), optional imports (Twitter/X likes, Goodreads, Pocket/Instapaper, Spotify artists), plus quick “swipe to place” taste test.

Cold-start edges: co-occurrence from public corpora + small curated ontologies; refine with behavior.

Visual Language (fast and legible)

Base: grayscale nodes/edges, low-stimulus.

Lenses:

Recency = hue.

Confidence = node border thickness.

Source type (text/pod/video) = glyph.

Emotional valence (optional) = subtle halo intensity.

Circuits: soft rounded capsules; drag to merge/split.

Session Flow (example)

Land on Self Map, see two warm clusters today.

Tap Bridge Seek → proposed bridge: “Causal ML ↔ Narrative arcs.”

Accept 1 content suggestion + 1 person-reveal invite (double-consent).

Check Local Pulse → “Interpretability tools quietly rising nearby.” Save as a Circuit.

Done. App signs you out with a nudge to return tomorrow.

Metrics That Matter

Bridge Rate: new cross-cluster edges per active user per week.

Quiet Corner Lift: % of sessions engaging with low-exposure, high-quality nodes.

Resonance Conversion: mutual reveals that lead to sustained exchange.

Curiosity Balance: Gini across clusters—lower is healthier variety.
